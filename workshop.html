New to the terminology?

Pimcore: software for managing product information, media, and content. DXP: digital experience platform. Instance: a separately running copy. Enrichment: adding useful information. Channel: a website, app, or other customer touchpoint. API: a connection through which software exchanges information. CDN: a service that delivers copies of files closer to visitors.

Customer identities, leads, and analytics are outside this content flow and need separate design.

Example: US updates a product specification → China imports the change → the local team reviews any affected Chinese copy → approved content reaches the selected channels. Local translations should not be silently overwritten.
