02 / Questions & touchpoints

The right questions.
A clearer direction.

Open a question to explore the touchpoints behind it. Capture what matters as you talk.

01What do we already have?+

Confirm the existing Pimcore version, content model, integrations, and operating team. Which material is ready to be reused?

02Which experience comes first?+

A website, a WeChat Mini Program, an Official Account, or a media portal? What should a customer be able to do?

03Who owns the local voice?+

Which information is globally controlled? What can China translate, adapt, and add? Who approves it, and how do updates preserve local work?

04How should the connection behave?+

What is synchronized, how often, and what happens when transfers fail? Plan local delivery, content removals, and review of changed translations.

05What makes it work in China?+

Who owns hosting, the operating entity, domains, applicable filings, WeChat accounts, support, and backups? Keep customer data and any overseas transfers as a separate design workstream.

06What is the smallest useful launch?+

Agree a first-release boundary, a decision owner, and success criteria before setting budget or timing commitments.
